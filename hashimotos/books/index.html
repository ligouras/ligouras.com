Hashimoto’s Thyroiditis: Lifestyle Interventions for Finding and Treating the Root Cause

Izabella Wentz, PharmD & Marta Nowosadzka, MD2015

The foundational root-cause guide: thyroid physiology, medications, nutrient depletions, the iodine controversy, gut permeability, infections, adrenal dysfunction, toxins, diets, supplements and lab testing.

Hashimoto’s Protocol: A 90-Day Plan for Reversing Thyroid Symptoms and Getting Your Life Back

Izabella Wentz, PharmD2017

A step-by-step 90-day plan with the Fundamental Protocols (Liver Support, Adrenal Recovery, Gut Balance), Advanced Protocols, self-assessments and checklists.

Hashimoto’s Food Pharmacology: Nutrition Protocols and Healing Recipes

Izabella Wentz, PharmD2019

Root Cause Intro, Paleo and Autoimmune diets, meal plans, digestive support, and 125 thyroid-supporting recipes with full nutritional analyses.

The Hashimoto’s AIP Cookbook: Easy Recipes for Thyroid Healing on the Paleo Autoimmune Protocol

Emily Kyle, MS, RDN, CLT, HCP & Phil Kyle, Chef2019

A practical AIP guide with a two-week meal plan, a food reintroduction guide, and over 100 five-ingredient, 30-minute, one-pot and make-ahead recipes.
